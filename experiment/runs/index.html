Metric definitions
  • Score: 100 × passed / (passed + failed) over all checks; a condition adds up the checks of all its runs. Skipped checks are left out.
  • Keyboard task: runs where a keyboard-only user can pick a color, open the size list and pick a size, and type a quantity without getting trapped.
  • Failed WCAG SC: distinct WCAG 2.2 success criteria with at least one failed check (mean per run).
  • APG widgets: widgets out of 10 with no failed check (mean per run).
  • Weighted failures: 3 × blockers (WCAG 2.1.1/2.1.2) + 2 × serious (other WCAG) + minor (APG only), mean per run.
  • axe miss %: share of failed checks that axe does not report.
  • Score / 10k tokens: run score divided by output tokens / 10,000 (mean per run).
